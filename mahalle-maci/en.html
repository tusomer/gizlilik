Mahalle Maçı privacy policy

This policy explains how Mahalle Maçı (the "game"), developed by Zone Oyun, handles personal data.

Data we collect

The game does not collect personal data and does not share any data with us:

  • There is no account, sign-in, or request for your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools.
  • The game does not ask for location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera permissions.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps your progress (team, settings, scores, in-game currency) only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google processes information such as downloads, updates and crash reports under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is designed for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; the game does not collect personal data at all.

Changes

If we add a feature such as ads, in-app purchases or accounts, we will update this policy beforehand and show the change on this page with its date.
